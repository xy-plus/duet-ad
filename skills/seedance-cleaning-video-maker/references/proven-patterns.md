# Patterns proven across three successful runs

These are structural patterns, not templates to copy verbatim. Rebuild descriptions from the current source frames.

| Pattern | Reference allocation | Best use | Cut rule | Observed result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Continuous causal demo | 9 states in one scene | One surface with stain creation, application, dwell, scrub, wipe, and result | No cuts | Successful 15-second 720 × 1280 output |
| Two-scene compression | 5 frames + 4 frames | A long montage with two related automotive interior cleaning loops | One direct cut after scene one finishes | Successful 15-second 720 × 1280 output |
| Three-scene viral montage | 3 + 3 + 3 frames | Three exceptionally strong before/action/after contrasts | Two direct cuts; no fourth scene | Successful 15-second 720 × 1280 output |

## Continuous causal demo lesson

Use all nine images to constrain the action chain when the source is already coherent. Remove subtitle-heavy intros even if they are visually energetic. Detailed causality is more valuable than extra scene variety.

## Two-scene compression lesson

For a source longer than 15 seconds, keep related scenes that share a product and visual language. Delete short fragments without a full result. A single explicit cut reduces object fusion and gives both scenes enough time for a readable wipe.

## Three-scene viral montage lesson

Choose subjects with different shapes and very clear contrast, such as a large wheel, a foamed panel, and a yellowed lamp. Three images per subject must carry before/action/after. The model can redistribute timing despite exact prompts, so verify the generated cut locations rather than assuming 5/5/5 seconds.

## Shared result characteristics

All three successful jobs used one Ark task, nine chronological image references, 15 seconds, 9:16, 720p, generated natural cleaning audio, and no watermark. The returned videos were approximately 15.04 seconds at 24 fps. Treat these as observed outputs, not guaranteed properties for future jobs.
